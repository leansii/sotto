# Sotto Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-07-09*

Sotto is built around one principle: **your conversations are nobody else's
business.**

## What Sotto collects

Nothing. Sotto has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry.
The extension makes no network requests to the internet.

## What Sotto processes, and where

- **Caption text** rendered by Google Meet or Zoom in your browser is read
  from the page and stored in `chrome.storage.local` — on your device only.
- **Call audio** (optional HQ mode) is captured from the call tab and your
  microphone and sent exclusively to `http://127.0.0.1` — a Whisper
  transcription server running on your own computer. Audio never leaves your
  machine and is discarded after transcription; only the resulting text is
  stored locally.
- **Exported transcripts** (.md / .txt) are plain files saved where you
  choose. Auto-saved transcripts go to your local Downloads folder.

## What Sotto shares

Nothing, with anyone, ever. There is no third party involved. The developer
has no access to any of your data.

## Data retention and deletion

Transcripts stay in your browser's local storage until you delete them — one
click in the extension popup, or uninstalling the extension, removes them.

## Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Sotto extension itself. Captions are generated by
Google Meet / Zoom as part of your call; whatever those platforms do with
your audio and captions is governed by their own policies. Sotto neither
adds to nor prevents it.
