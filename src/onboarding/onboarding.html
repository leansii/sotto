Sotto keeps transcripts of your calls.
Here is exactly what that means.

What is captured

Live captions that Google Meet / Zoom render in your browser, and — only when you explicitly press «Start HQ» — the call audio and your microphone for local AI transcription.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Transcripts are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. The extension makes no requests to the internet; in HQ mode audio is sent only to 127.0.0.1 — your own computer. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.

Other people are part of your calls

Sotto can post a short notice into the meeting chat so participants know a transcript is being kept (enabled by default, toggle in the popup). Laws on recording conversations differ by country — complying with them is your responsibility.

You can review everything above at any time: the extension is open source (github.com/leansii/sotto), and this page never expires — capture stays off until you enable it here.
